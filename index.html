Bơ & Mia Content Poster

A personal publishing tool for one animated cat couple.

Bơ & Mia Content Poster is a single-account app that uploads the developer's own animated short videos to the developer's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Not a public service. This app has no sign-up, no other users, and no interface offered to third parties. It exists to automate one creator's own publishing workflow for one TikTok account.

What it does

  • Connects, via TikTok OAuth, to the developer's own TikTok account only.
  • Uploads pre-made video files (with caption and cover image) using the Content Posting API.
  • Runs on a schedule the developer controls no public dashboard, no bot replies, no automated engagement.

About the series

Bơ & Mia is an original animated 3D short-video series following two cats Bơ (protective, a little clumsy) and Mia (playful, quick-witted) through daily life together. Episodes are produced by the developer and published under this app.
