In plain terms: Bơ & Mia Content Poster is a single-user tool built by one independent creator to publish their own original animated short videos to their own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. It is not offered as a service to the public and does not post on behalf of anyone else.

01What this is

Bơ & Mia Content Poster ("the App") is a personal content-publishing tool developed and operated by an individual creator (the "Developer") for the "Bơ & Mia" animated short-video series. The App uses TikTok's Content Posting API to upload video files, together with a caption and cover image, to the Developer's own connected TikTok account.

The App is a private, single-account automation tool. It has no public sign-up, no other user accounts, and no interface offered to third parties.

04Content & disclosure

All video content posted through the App is original animated content created for the Bơ & Mia series, generated with AI creative tools and reviewed by the Developer before publishing. Every post goes through a preview step where the Developer can review, edit the caption/cover, and confirm before it is published — the App does not publish silently without that confirmation.

Where required by TikTok's policies, posts are labeled to disclose that they contain AI-generated or AI-assisted content.
