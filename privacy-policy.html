In plain terms: Bơ & Mia Content Poster only ever connects to one TikTok account — the Developer's own. It doesn't have other users, doesn't run ads or trackers, and doesn't sell or share data. The only data it touches is what's needed to publish the Developer's own videos.

01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data Bơ & Mia Content Poster (the "App") accesses when it uses TikTok's Content Posting API, and how that data is handled. The App is a single-account personal tool built and operated by an individual creator (the "Developer") solely to publish the Developer's own animated video content to the Developer's own TikTok account.

02Data we access

DataWhy
TikTok OAuth access token (video.publish scope)Authorizes the App to publish video posts to the Developer's own TikTok account.
Basic TikTok account identifiers (username, account ID) returned by the APIConfirms which account a post is being published to.
Video files, captions, and cover images created by the DeveloperThe actual content being uploaded and published.

03What we don't collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or data from any user other than the Developer.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers.
  • No browsing history, device data, or location data.
  • No access to TikTok direct messages, followers' data, or other accounts' content.

04How data is used

The OAuth token and account identifiers are used exclusively to authenticate API requests and confirm a post is going to the correct, Developer-owned account. Video files, captions, and cover images are used exclusively to create the post the Developer has reviewed and approved. None of this data is used for any other purpose, such as profiling, advertising, or analytics.

05Storage & retention

Access tokens are stored only for as long as needed to operate the App and are kept confidential. Video files are held only for the time needed to prepare and publish a post, then removed from any temporary working storage. The App does not maintain a public-facing database of user data, because it has no users other than the Developer.

06Sharing with third parties

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. The only external service the App communicates with is TikTok's own API, for the sole purpose of publishing the Developer's posts, governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

07Your rights

Because the App has no third-party users, the data described here belongs to the Developer alone. The Developer can revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok's account settings, which immediately invalidates the App's ability to post or access account data.

08Changes to this policy

This document may be updated as the App's functionality changes. The version number and effective date above will be updated whenever a material change is made.
